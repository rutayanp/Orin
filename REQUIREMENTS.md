# Requirements (proposed)

- Voice-first iPhone client with text, photos, files, share sheet and correction; server-side AI.
- Local Whisper, Piper, OpenClaw and replaceable Nemotron/Qwen models.
- Persistent lists, contextual updates, undo, timers, alarms, reminders, weather and wireless printing.
- Receipt recognition, purchase history, document memory, user-controlled proactive assistance.
- Offline core, authenticated transport, backups, recovery, no paid AI APIs, no RAG without evidence.
- Portable Linux CPU development and Jetson ARM64/CUDA deployment; native iOS builds separately.
- Acceptance: exact state changes, restart persistence, duplicate prevention, ambiguity handling, offline behavior and measured Jetson latency/memory. See ARCHITECTURE.md for proposed targets.
